## 📥 DOWNLOAD INSTRUCTIONS

### Method 1: University Access (Best)
1. Connect to your university VPN
2. Visit IEEE Xplore / ScienceDirect / SpringerLink
3. Download PDFs directly (legitimate access)

### Method 2: Google Scholar
1. Search paper title on https://scholar.google.com
2. Look for **[PDF]** links on the right side
3. These often link to author homepages or institutional repos

### Method 3: Sci-Hub (if legal in your country)
1. Use links provided above with sci-hub.se
2. Paste DOI into Sci-Hub search
3. Download PDF

### Method 4: Author Contact
1. Find author email from recent papers
2. Email requesting PDF (authors usually happy to share)
3. Most researchers share their work freely
