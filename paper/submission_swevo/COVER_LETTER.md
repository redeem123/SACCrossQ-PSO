# Cover Letter -- Swarm and Evolutionary Computation

**To:** The Editor-in-Chief, *Swarm and Evolutionary Computation*  
**Date:** April 13, 2026  
**Re:** Submission of "Soft Actor-Critic with Attractor-Field PSO Parameter Adaptation for UAV Path Planning"

Dear Editor,

I am writing on behalf of my coauthors, Thanh-Hung Nguyen and Duc-Toan Nguyen, to submit the manuscript titled **"Soft Actor-Critic with Attractor-Field PSO Parameter Adaptation for UAV Path Planning"** for consideration in *Swarm and Evolutionary Computation*.

This manuscript studies reinforcement-learning-guided parameter adaptation for particle swarm optimization in UAV path planning. The paper presents AFSACPSO, which combines a compact 15-dimensional temporal-summary swarm state with a 9-dimensional attractor-field latent action that expands into particle-wise PSO coefficients. The empirical contribution is intentionally scoped to the evidence currently supported by the repository: the manuscript separates the stronger frozen external deployment variant (`SACPSO with global mode`) from the proposed attractor-field control-interface study (`AFSACPSO`) and reports a matched same-backbone ablation to isolate the role of control granularity. The results show that the compact-state SAC pipeline improves on earlier frozen SAC/DDPG baselines, while the attractor-field interface remains competitive with the matched global controller and outperforms higher-dimensional SAC control variants on the easier scenarios.

We believe the paper fits the scope of *Swarm and Evolutionary Computation* because it contributes a structured adaptive-control formulation for swarm intelligence, evaluates it under a reproducible benchmark protocol, and frames the findings conservatively relative to strong classical adaptive PSO baselines. The submission should be of interest to readers working on adaptive parameter control, reinforcement-learning-guided metaheuristics, and UAV path planning under constrained three-dimensional environments.

This manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration by any other journal. All authors have approved the manuscript and declare that they have no competing interests.

Thank you for your consideration.
