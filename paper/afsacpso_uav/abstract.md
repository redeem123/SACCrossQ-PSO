AFSACPSO is the paper's proposed attractor-field control-interface design for SAC-guided PSO parameter adaptation in UAV path planning. The retained implementation uses a 15D swarm-summary state, a 9D latent SAC action, and an attractor-field mapping that expands the latent action into particle-wise inertia and acceleration coefficients.

The current evidence supports a two-level reading. In the internal same-backbone frozen-policy ablation, AFSACPSO remains competitive with the matched global SAC controller and outperforms the fixed-subgroup and direct per-particle variants on the easier scenarios. For external RL and classical comparisons, however, the strongest retained frozen deployment row is SACPSO-Global, which is therefore the correct representative SAC baseline for the paper's main benchmark tables.
